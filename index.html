An edge node is a computing device placed close to a data source. It filters data locally and sends only what matters to the cloud, which cuts traffic and delay. This site runs a real edge-node experiment and shows what it measured.

Run an experiment to see the processing time.

The threshold decides the saving

Readings are spread evenly from 20 to 45°C, so 10 of every 25 degrees exceed 35°C, which gives an expected alert rate of 40%. Raise the threshold and savings grow; lower it and they shrink.

Not proven here

Data is simulated and nothing is sent over a real network, so latency, energy and true bandwidth savings still need a physical test.
